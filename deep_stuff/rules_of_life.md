# 10 Commandments of Life

1. "Anything that can go wrong will go wrong."- Murphy's law

2. "We never take credit for our best 
skills, for they come without effort and privilege."- Rochard Muller

3. Fail. Fail faster and better. 

10. "Grief is the price we pay for love." - Dr. Colin Murray Parkes

5. "With great power comes great responsibility."- Uncle Ben

6. Comparison is the key to sadness and narcissism, and academia thrives on comparison.

7. Capitalism doesn't value productivity. It values privilege and is antithetical to love, loneliness, and boredom.

8. Learning is hard, but learning from mistakes is much harder and unlearning is the hardest. 

9. "To live, is to suffer." This is the meaning of life. - Friedrich Nietzsche

10. Attachment is the root of all sadness and anxiety.
