# 10 Commandments of Life

1. Anything that can go wrong will go wrong.- Murphy's law

2. We never take credit for our best skills, for they come without effort and privilege.- Richard Muller

3. Fail. Fail faster and better. 

4. Grief is the price we pay for love - Dr. Colin Murray Parkes

5. With great power comes great responsibility - Uncle Ben

6. Comparison is the key to sadness and narcissism, and academia thrives on comparison.

7. Capitalism is antithetical to love, loneliness, and boredom.

8. Learning is hard, but learning from mistakes is much harder. 

9. To live, is to suffer. This is the meaning of life. - Friedrich Nietzsche

10. Attachment is the root cause of sadness and anxiety.
